# 《王冠》 / The Crown — store copy

## Positioning

「一个会问你“多少才够”的骑砍式沙盘」

“A sandbox strategy game that keeps asking how much is enough.”

## 简体中文

### 短描述

带着五个弟兄流落河湾，在一张活着的宣纸舆图上招兵、受雇、夺城、称王。你每一幕都要说出“多少才够”——游戏只记下答案，也记下你后来做了什么。

### 关于游戏

边军解散，王座虚悬。你从流浪佣兵起步，在领主战争、围城与流民匪队之间寻找下一笔军饷。酒馆合同把你带入诸侯的战争；第一块封地让边境的烽火第一次与你有关；王冠则把所有人的矛头转向你。

《王冠》是一款短篇、可重复游玩的沙盘策略游戏。世界按种子确定性运转：领主沿官道行军，城镇易手，派系结盟与开战。战斗保留简洁的兵种克制、阵型与指令选择，并用水墨队列、箭雨和溃逃演出既定结算。

每一幕，游戏会问一次“够了”是多少。它不判断、不劝告；结局的诏书只把你的承诺、编年史和实际选择并排放回你面前。

### 特点

- 从五人小队到自立称王的四幕短篇战役
- 活着的派系战争、城镇经济、围城与官道路网
- 民兵、老兵、匪徒三类底色与枪、弓、骑三种军备
- 三名可招揽部将，各有被动能力和个人事件
- 同一种子可复现的世界与独立的战斗演出随机流
- 中文 / English；鼠标、触屏与桌面全屏

## English

### Short description

Begin with five soldiers on a living paper map. Recruit, take contracts, seize a fief, and found a kingdom—while the game remembers every time you said what would be enough.

### About this game

The frontier army is gone and the throne stands empty. You begin as a wandering mercenary, hunting the next wage payment between lordly wars, sieges, and roaming bandits. Tavern contracts pull you into faction conflicts. Your first fief makes the border fires personal. A crown turns every spear toward you.

The Crown is a compact, replayable sandbox strategy campaign. Its seeded world advances deterministically: lords travel the road network, towns change hands, and factions make war. Battles combine readable counters, formations, and commands with ink-figure formations, arrow volleys, and rout choreography that present the resolved simulation.

Once per act, the game asks what “enough” means. It never judges or advises. At the end, an edict simply places your promises, chronicle, and actions beside one another.

### Features

- A four-act arc from a five-person company to a self-founded kingdom
- Living faction wars, town economies, sieges, and a road-bound campaign map
- Militia, veteran, and bandit backgrounds equipped as spear, archer, or cavalry
- Three hireable lieutenants with passives and personal event cards
- Deterministic seeded simulation with a separate presentation random stream
- Chinese / English; mouse, touch, windowed, and fullscreen play

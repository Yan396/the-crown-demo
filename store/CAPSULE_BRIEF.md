# Steam capsule brief — 兵戈舆图

## One read at thumbnail size

A torn-ink campaign map seen from above. Three black road lines converge beneath
a square cinnabar **帥** seal; faint troop columns and one threatened town sit in
the paper grain. The seal is the only saturated element. The title 《王冠》 is
large, vertical-capable, and legible without the subtitle.

## Composition system

- Hero: the existing campaign-map **帥** seal, enlarged but still visibly stamped.
- Ground: warm xuanzhi paper, existing ink mountains/roads/town marks; no fantasy
  painting and no emoji.
- Tension: two ink armies enter from opposite edges; the road junction and seal
  form a single visual triangle.
- Accent: existing cinnabar token only. Do not add gold gradients, blue magic,
  photoreal faces, crowns, or unimplemented troop types.
- Logo lockup: 《王冠》 over `THE CROWN`; positioning line is optional only in
  wide crops.
- Leave safe negative space around the seal and wordmark so the master can crop
  to header, small, main, and library capsule ratios without regenerating art.

## Deliverables for the artist

One layered 4K landscape master plus transparent wordmark and seal layers.
Export every Steam capsule from that master at the dimensions currently shown
in Steamworks, checking the smallest crop first. Also export a square source
icon for Tauri/itch packaging. No screenshot compositing and no rating badges.
